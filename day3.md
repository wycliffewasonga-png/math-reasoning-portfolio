### Problem 1 (Algebra)

Solve: 4x + 8 = 24

Solution:
4x = 16
x = 4

Explanation:
Subtract 8 and divide by 4.

---

### Problem 2 (Algebra)

Solve: 6x = 30

Solution:
x = 5

Explanation:
Divide both sides by 6.

---

### Problem 3 (Logic)

Statement:
All cats are animals. Some animals are wild.
Are all cats wild?

Answer:
No.

Explanation:
The statement does not guarantee that cats belong to the group of wild animals.

---

### Problem 4 (Logic)

Statement:
All programmers solve problems. Wycliffe is a programmer.
Does Wycliffe solve problems?

Answer:
Yes.

Explanation:
Since all programmers solve problems and Wycliffe is a programmer, he must solve problems.
